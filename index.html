Your trident maple

Acer buergerianum

Ramification counts how many times each primary branch has divided into fine twigs: 3 means about 8 twigs per branch, 5 about 32. It rises as buds break along existing twigs. Pinch long shoots, cut back to a bud, defoliate a strong tree in early summer, and wire branches out flat so light reaches inside.
